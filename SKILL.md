## Panel-specific overlay

Use naive flat storybook shapes with crayon/pastel/pencil grain and imperfect hand lines. Surround the subject with one irregular white hand-cut backing of varying width and visible area, never a uniform outline. Keep a small stamp-like focus, extensive whitespace and 2–4 warm source-derived colours. Avoid smooth vectors, realistic volume and cheap cartoon styling.

These overlay rules add to `references/soldier-runtime.md`. They never replace the source brief or the family runtime contract.
